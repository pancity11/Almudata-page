Security

Built to protect your wallet the way you'd protect it yourself.

Biometric authorization

Fingerprint and PIN work as fully independent ways to authorize a transaction, so you're never locked out of your own wallet.

Protected virtual account

Your dedicated funding account is created and credited through a webhook system with duplicate protection, so a repeated transfer or webhook never credits your wallet twice.

Honest transaction status

A purchase is only ever marked "Completed" once it's explicitly confirmed — network failures are distinguished from real rejections, so your history stays accurate.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is my money safe in the wallet?

Yes. Every transaction is authorized with your PIN or fingerprint, and wallet funding runs through a webhook system with duplicate protection, so a repeated transfer never credits you twice.

What happens if a network fails during a purchase?

Pancity distinguishes a network failure from a confirmed rejection, so your transaction is never marked "Completed" unless it's actually been confirmed by the provider. Contact support if a transaction is stuck and it'll be resolved.

Do I have to pay to become a reseller?

No. There's no activation fee or hidden charge. You get wholesale pricing the moment you sign up.

How do I fund my wallet?

Every account comes with its own dedicated virtual account number. Transfer to it from any Nigerian bank and your wallet is credited instantly.
