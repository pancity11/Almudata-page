Common questions.

Is my money safe in the wallet?

Yes. Every transaction is authorized with your PIN or fingerprint, and wallet funding runs through a webhook system with duplicate protection, so a repeated transfer never credits you twice.

What happens if a network fails during a purchase?

Almu Data Sub distinguishes a network failure from a confirmed rejection, so your transaction is never marked "Completed" unless the provider has actually confirmed it. If a transaction is stuck, contact support and it will be resolved.

Do I have to pay to become a reseller?

No. There's no activation fee or hidden charge. You get wholesale pricing the moment you sign up.

How do I fund my wallet?

Every account comes with its own dedicated virtual account number. Transfer to it from any Nigerian bank and your wallet is credited instantly.
