Delete your account

You can remove your Almu Data Sub account and personal data at any time. Choose the in-app method for the fastest result, or send us a request below.

OPTION 1 · IN THE APP

Delete from your device

  1. Open the Almu Data Sub app and sign in
  2. Go to Profile → Account Settings
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm with your transaction PIN
  4. Your account is deactivated immediately and queued for permanent deletion
or request it here

OPTION 2 · REQUEST FORM

Ask us to delete it for you

This opens your email app addressed to support@almudatasub.com. We verify your identity before deleting anything.

WHAT GETS DELETED

Removed immediately: profile details, saved beneficiaries, app preferences and login access.

Kept for a period: wallet transaction records are retained as required under Nigerian financial regulations, then deleted. Any wallet balance is refunded to your bank account before closure.
